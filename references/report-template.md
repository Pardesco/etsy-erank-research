# REPORT.md template

Fill every number from `04_results/findings.json` or `research.xlsx`. Cut any
section with no data (for example, Style Matrix when phase 1 was skipped).
Plain sentences, short tables, no hype.

```markdown
# <Niche> on Etsy: eRank research (<date>, <country>)

**Workbook:** `04_results/research.xlsx` · **Seeds:** <n> · **Related keywords:** <unique> · **Top listings:** <rows>

## What to do next
1. <Make X, titled with "<phrase>": <searches>/mo, <competition> listings, KD <kd>.>
2. <Retitle Y: "<seed>" → "<better phrase>" (<n>× the searches, lower KD).>
3. <Avoid Z: <reason, e.g. crowded or IP>.>

## 1. Crowded vs. open
<One sentence.> Table: seed | searches/mo | listings | per 1k | KD, top 5 and bottom 5 seeds.

## 2. What sells on Etsy vs. what eRank says   (only if phase 1 ran)
<Which badge-heavy styles are crowded, which badge-light styles have room.>

## 3. Long-tail opportunities

## 4. Better phrasing
Table from Better Phrasing: seed → phrase, with both volumes and KDs.

## 5. Price and format
Median digital vs. physical price, % digital, any seed where one format dominates.

The 8-10 most-used tags, with eRank volume where known.

How many of the top 50 raw opportunities were flagged, with 3-5 examples. One line: the screen is a keyword filter, not legal advice.

eRank estimates; competition counts dated by eRank; est. sales are not Etsy data.
```

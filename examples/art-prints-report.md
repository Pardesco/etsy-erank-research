# Art prints on Etsy: eRank research (2026-10-18, USA)

An example REPORT.md from one run of this skill. It is shortened, and the
numbers are eRank estimates from that day.

**Seeds:** 55 from 22 styles on Etsy "art prints" pages 1-5 · **Related keywords:** 15,574 unique · **Top listings:** 5,464

## What to do next
1. Make pressed-flower prints and title them with "pressed flower print": the seed "pressed flower art" gets 1,583 searches a month against 19,897 listings (80 per 1,000), but 91% of its top listings are real pressed flowers, so the print wording matters.
2. Retitle Japanese-style prints: "japanese art print" (621/mo) → "japanese wall art" (2,693/mo).
3. List a 2027 art calendar now: "2027 wall calendar" gets 313 searches a month against 43 listings, KD 1.
4. Avoid Frame TV art and mega-bundles unless you already rank: they win the badges, but the competition is the heaviest.

## 2. What sells vs. what eRank says
The styles with the most Bestseller / Popular now badges (seasonal and Frame TV art, flat digital illustration, mega-bundles) have the worst demand-to-supply ratios. Badges show what sells for shops that already rank, not how hard it is for a new listing to be seen.

## 5. Price and format
52% of top listings are digital downloads. Medians: $6.50 digital, $32.59 physical. Frame TV art is all digital at about $5. Linocut is 97% physical at about $24.

## 6. Tags the top listings share
printable wall art (886 listings), digital download (732), wall art, gallery wall set, minimalist wall art, instant download, housewarming gift (10,081 searches/mo).

## 7. IP warning
11 of the top 50 raw opportunities were film, game or living-artist names (Dune, Arcane, One Piece, Yayoi Kusama, Hiroshi Nagai). They are flagged and kept out of the clean list. The screen is a keyword filter, not legal advice.

## Caveats
eRank volumes are estimates, competition counts carry eRank's own dates, and est. sales are view-based estimates, not Etsy data.
